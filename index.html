Formula Sheet (Levels 1–3)

  • Remainder theorem: remainder of \(f(x)\div(x-k)\) is \(f(k)\)
  • Factor theorem: \(f(k)=0 \iff (x-k)\) is a factor of \(f(x)\)
  • Long division: \(f(x)=(x-k)Q(x)+f(k)\)
  • Division form: \(f(x)=(\text{divisor})(\text{quotient})+\text{remainder}\)
  • Roots from factors: \((x-r)\) is a factor \(\iff x=r\) is a root
  • Discriminant: \(ax^2+bx+c=0\): \(D=b^2-4ac\)
  • Rational roots: \(D\ge 0\) & \(D\) a perfect square
  • Irrational roots: \(D>0\) but \(D\) not a perfect square
  • No real roots: \(D<0\)
  • Difference of squares: \(a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)\)
  • Perfect square: \((x\pm a)^2=x^2\pm 2ax+a^2\)
